Top Tourist Places

🏔 Manali

Manali is one of India's most famous hill stations located in Himachal Pradesh. Surrounded by snow-covered mountains, rivers, waterfalls and adventure sports, it is perfect for honeymooners and nature lovers.

🌄 Kullu

Kullu Valley is known as the Valley of Gods. It offers breathtaking landscapes, green forests, rafting, trekking and beautiful apple orchards.

🛕 Kedarnath

Kedarnath Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Located amidst the Himalayas, it attracts millions of pilgrims every year.

☕ Chikmagalur

Chikmagalur in Karnataka is famous for lush coffee plantations, misty hills, waterfalls, wildlife and peaceful surroundings.

🌊 Mangalore

Mangalore is a beautiful coastal city with pristine beaches, historic temples, churches, delicious seafood and rich culture.

🏞 Leh

Leh is the capital of Ladakh and is famous for monasteries, mountain passes, scenic valleys and thrilling bike trips.

❄ Ladakh

Ladakh is known as the Land of High Passes. It offers crystal-clear lakes, snowy mountains, Buddhist monasteries, Nubra Valley and Pangong Lake.

🌿 Explore More

India is full of incredible destinations including Kashmir, Goa, Ooty, Coorg, Jaipur, Rishikesh, Shimla, Darjeeling, Andaman Islands and many more.
